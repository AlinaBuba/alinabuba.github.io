Specialisations

Topics that adults, teenagers and parents of children bring to me. If your request is not here, write to me and we will see whether I can help.

Emotional state and psychological difficulties

Common requests

  • Anxiety, excessive worry, constant tension
  • Low mood, apathy, loss of motivation
  • Emotional exhaustion, stress and difficulty adapting
  • Self-doubt, low self-esteem, self-criticism
  • Difficulty understanding and regulating emotions

How we work

Together we explore what is behind the state: we notice the link between thoughts, emotions and behaviour and choose practical ways to improve it. When needed, we use psychodiagnostic methods.

Self-discovery and personal growth

Common requests

  • Exploring personality features
  • Understanding your needs, values and behavioural reactions
  • Difficulty making decisions
  • Personal crises and searching for direction

How we work

We explore your features, needs and values, look at options for decisions and search for direction at a pace that is comfortable for you.

Cognitive functions and neuropsychology

Common requests

  • Difficulty concentrating and sustaining attention
  • Features of memory, thinking and cognitive activity
  • Problems with self-organisation, planning and behavioural control
  • Neuropsychological assessment of cognitive functions

How we work

We start with a conversation and, when needed, a neuropsychological assessment of cognitive functions. Based on it we discuss what can be done next. The assessment does not replace a medical diagnosis.

Child and adolescent psychology

Common requests

  • Difficulties with attention, memory and learning
  • Features of cognitive development in children
  • Difficulties with self-regulation and behaviour
  • Questions related to attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)
  • Neuropsychological diagnostics and choosing directions for corrective work

How we work

Neuropsychological diagnostics helps understand a child’s developmental features and choose directions for corrective work. A medical diagnosis, including ADHD, is made by a doctor, not a psychologist.

What is outside my work

  • Acute psychiatric conditions (psychosis, severe depression with suicidal thoughts).
  • Treatment of addictions.
  • Prescribing or stopping medication. Only a psychiatrist can do this.

In such cases I will help you find the right specialist.

Is therapy right for me?

Therapy suits you if you are ready for regular meetings and small tasks between them, and want to understand yourself better. You do not need a “serious” reason: it is enough to feel that things are hard as they are.
