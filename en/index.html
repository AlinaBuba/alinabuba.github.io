Does this sound familiar?

  • You wake up already tired, before the day has even begun.
  • Your mind loops the same thoughts, and at night they get louder.
  • It is hard to concentrate, and it feels like your memory lets you down.
  • You can do a lot, but rarely let yourself stop.
  • It is hard to say “no” or to ask for help.
  • You worry about how your child learns, focuses or behaves.

If you recognise yourself, it does not mean something is “wrong” with you. It is a reason to give yourself support.

What I work with

Emotional wellbeing

Anxiety, low mood, burnout, self-criticism, difficulty regulating emotions.

Self-discovery

Your needs and values, difficult decisions, personal crises, finding direction.

Cognitive functions

Concentration, memory, self-organisation, planning. Neuropsychological assessment.

Children and teens

Attention, memory, learning, self-regulation, ADHD. Neuropsychological diagnostics.

How we will work

  1. Intro call

    15 minutes, free. You tell me what brings you here, and I explain how I work. No obligation.

  2. First session

    We clarify your request and agree on goals and format. You decide what to talk about.

  3. Regular work

    Usually weekly meetings. Together we see what works and adjust the plan when needed.

A few words about me

I am a clinical psychologist and neuropsychologist with a master’s degree in clinical psychology with fundamentals of psychotherapy (Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv). I draw on clinical psychology, modern psychodiagnostic methods and the principles of cognitive-behavioural therapy.

It matters to me to create a space where you can speak openly about your experiences without fear of judgement.
