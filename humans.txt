/* HOW IT WAS MADE */
The site is generated, not written by hand. tools/build.py (template, SEO,
settings) and tools/content.py (UA/EN page text) produce every HTML page,
sitemap.xml, robots.txt and site.webmanifest. tools/images.py optimises
photos to WebP and renders the favicon, app icons and og-image with
headless Chrome. tools/fetch_fonts.py downloads the font subsets.
tools/check.py verifies internal links, hreflang, JSON-LD and the absence
of horizontal scroll; tools/shots.py takes desktop and phone screenshots.
Common commands live in the justfile (just build, serve, shot, check).
Do not edit the generated HTML by hand: change the tools/ sources and rebuild.
